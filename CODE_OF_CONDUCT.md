# Code of conduct

DHCPulse welcomes respectful, technically grounded participation from people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and implementations rather than people.
- Respect privacy and use synthetic examples.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and project direction.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and knowingly submitting customer data, credentials, or other sensitive material are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not use a public issue to report conduct concerns. Contact the repository owner through an established private channel. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens people, project safety, or the privacy boundary.
